## Introduction

Florence ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence)) is the historic city of Tuscany in central Italy and the capital of the province of Firenze. It sits on the Arno River ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arno)) and became one of the most influential cities in Europe because of its wealth, politics, and culture. that Florence was founded as a Roman colony in the 1st century BCE, later became a republic, and then served as a duchy and later as a capital of modern Italy.

Its importance rests not only on its past but also on its lasting cultural legacy. Florence gave the world major artistic and intellectual figures such as [[Leonardo da Vinci]], [[Michelangelo]], [[Dante]], Machiavelli, and [[Galileo]]. It also helped shape the Renaissance ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renaissance)), produced the Florentine vernacular that became modern Italian, and gave its name to the florin, a once influential coin.

## History

Florence began as a Roman military colony in the 1st century BCE and became a provincial capital of the Roman Empire by the 3rd century CE. During the Middle Ages it was ruled by different foreign powers, but from the late 10th century onward it grew in wealth and importance. Under Countess Matilda of Tuscany, it became a leading city in the region.

The city’s political structure became distinctive in 1293, when the Ordinances of Justice were adopted. This system limited the power of nobles and labourers and made office rotation more frequent. How the rise of the wool industry and banking helped create a wealthy merchant class that gradually dominated the city’s politics.
